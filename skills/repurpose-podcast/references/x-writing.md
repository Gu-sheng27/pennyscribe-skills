# X writing

Write for scanning, discussion, and link-through without imitating Xiaohongshu copy.

- Generate one concise standalone post and, when the material warrants it, one 4–8 item thread.
- Put the episode link in the standalone post and in the final thread item unless the user requests another placement.
- Lead with a concrete claim, tension, or surprising implication. Avoid generic hooks such as “This changed everything.”
- Prefer short paragraphs and one idea per thread item.
- Do not invent the guest's X handle or use unrelated hashtags.
- Keep every factual claim grounded in cited paragraph IDs.
- Provide the finished copy, not writing advice or labels inside the post.
