# Xiaohongshu writing and cards

Generate a native Xiaohongshu package: title options, caption, and a visual story of 4–8 portrait cards.

## Narrative

Use this sequence when it fits the episode:

1. Cover: one specific promise or tension.
2. Context: why this episode matters.
3. Core insights: one idea per card.
4. Quote or counterintuitive point.
5. Practical takeaway.
6. Closing card with source attribution and listening invitation.

Avoid clickbait that overstates the transcript. Keep card body copy short enough to breathe. Put longer nuance in the caption.

## Themes

- `editorial-red`: red, black, and warm paper; Chinese editorial character.
- `cream-notes`: cream paper, annotations, calm lifestyle tone.
- `neo-brutal`: bold high-contrast blocks for technology and business.
- `dark-cinema`: dark photographic treatment for interviews and profiles.

Choose one theme based on tone. Do not mix themes within a set. Use the episode cover only when it is available and legally exposed by the source page. Never imply AI-generated decoration is an original episode image.

## Grounding

Attach source paragraph IDs to insight and quote cards. Use quotation marks only for `verbatim` or `lightly_edited` text. Keep the original episode URL in the caption or closing card.
